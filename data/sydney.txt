Sydney, Australia's largest city, is famous for its stunning harbor, iconic Opera House, and beautiful beaches.

Airports:
Sydney is served by Sydney Kingsford Smith Airport (SYD), located about 8 km south of the city center. It's Australia's busiest airport and handles both domestic and international flights.

Transportation from airport:
The Airport Link train connects the airport to the city center in about 13 minutes. A single ticket costs around 19.50 AUD to Central Station. The train also connects to other parts of the city. Airport buses and shuttles are available, typically costing 15-20 AUD. Taxis cost approximately 45-55 AUD to the city center, depending on traffic and destination.

Getting around Sydney:
Sydney's public transport system includes trains, buses, ferries, and light rail. The Opal card is a smart card that works on all modes of transport. You can purchase and top up Opal cards at stations, convenience stores, and online. Fares are calculated based on distance and mode, with daily and weekly caps.

The Sydney Trains network covers the metropolitan area. A single journey within the city center costs around 3-4 AUD. The network is extensive and connects to suburbs and outer areas.

Ferries are a scenic way to travel, especially to destinations like Manly, Taronga Zoo, and Watsons Bay. Ferry rides offer beautiful harbor views and are part of the Opal network.

Buses complement the train network and are essential for reaching areas not served by trains. The light rail connects the city center to the eastern suburbs and inner west.

Taxis and ride-sharing services like Uber are widely available. Sydney also has bike-sharing programs.

Major attractions:
The Sydney Opera House is one of the world's most recognizable buildings. Guided tours are available, and you can also attend performances. The exterior can be viewed for free from various vantage points around Circular Quay.

The Sydney Harbour Bridge offers the BridgeClimb experience for panoramic views, though it's expensive (around 300+ AUD). You can also walk across the bridge for free on the pedestrian walkway.

Bondi Beach is one of Australia's most famous beaches, known for its golden sand and excellent surf. The Bondi to Coogee coastal walk is a beautiful 6 km trail with stunning ocean views.

The Royal Botanic Gardens offer peaceful green space in the heart of the city, with beautiful harbor views. Admission is free.

Taronga Zoo is located on the north shore and offers great views of the harbor along with its animal exhibits. Tickets cost around 47 AUD for adults.

The Rocks is Sydney's historic area, with cobblestone streets, markets, pubs, and museums. It's a great place to learn about Sydney's colonial history.

Accommodation:
Sydney offers accommodation for all budgets, though prices are generally high. Hostels start around 30-50 AUD per night. Budget hotels cost 100-150 AUD, mid-range hotels 150-300 AUD, and luxury hotels can exceed 400 AUD per night.

Popular areas to stay include Circular Quay (convenient but expensive), The Rocks (historic, central), Darling Harbour (modern, good for families), and Bondi (beachside, more relaxed).

Food and dining:
Sydney's food scene is diverse and excellent, reflecting its multicultural population. Seafood is particularly good, with fresh fish and chips, Sydney rock oysters, and Moreton Bay bugs.

A meal at a casual restaurant typically costs 20-35 AUD. Fine dining restaurants can cost 100-200+ AUD per person. The city has excellent Asian cuisine, especially Thai, Vietnamese, and Chinese.

Café culture is strong, with excellent coffee (AUD 4-6 for a flat white or cappuccino). Brunch is a big part of Sydney culture, with many cafes offering creative breakfast and lunch options.

Fish markets are popular, especially the Sydney Fish Market, where you can buy fresh seafood or eat at the many restaurants and stalls.

Practical tips:
Sydney uses Australian Dollars (AUD). Credit cards are widely accepted, and contactless payment is common.

The best times to visit are spring (September-November) and fall (March-May) when weather is pleasant. Summer (December-February) can be hot and humid, while winter (June-August) is mild but can be rainy.

Sydney is generally safe, but be cautious in some areas at night, especially around Kings Cross and certain parts of the CBD.

Tipping is not expected but appreciated for good service. Rounding up or leaving 10% is common at restaurants.

The sun is very strong in Australia. Always wear sunscreen, a hat, and sunglasses when outdoors.

Many attractions offer online booking with discounts. The Sydney Pass can save money if you plan to visit multiple paid attractions.

Public transport can be crowded during rush hours (7-9 AM and 5-7 PM). The Opal card caps daily and weekly fares, making it economical for frequent travel.
