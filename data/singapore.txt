Singapore, a city-state in Southeast Asia, is known for its cleanliness, efficiency, modern architecture, and diverse culture.

Airports:
Singapore is served by Singapore Changi Airport (SIN), consistently ranked as one of the world's best airports. It's located about 20 km northeast of the city center and is a destination in itself, with gardens, shopping, and entertainment.

Transportation from airport:
The MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) connects the airport to the city center via the East West Line. The journey takes about 30-40 minutes and costs around 2-3 SGD. Airport shuttles are available. Taxis are metered and cost approximately 20-40 SGD to the city center, depending on destination and time of day. Uber and Grab (Southeast Asian ride-sharing) operate but are less common than taxis.

Getting around Singapore:
Singapore's MRT system is extensive, efficient, and clean. It covers most of the city and is the best way to get around. A single journey costs 0.83-2.08 SGD depending on distance. The EZ-Link card or NETS FlashPay card can be used for MRT, buses, and some taxis.

Buses complement the MRT network and cover areas not served by the metro. They use the same card system and are air-conditioned.

Taxis are plentiful, metered, and relatively affordable. They're clean and efficient. Ride-sharing services like Grab are available.

Walking is pleasant in many areas, especially in the city center, Marina Bay, and along the waterfront.

Major attractions:
Marina Bay Sands is an iconic integrated resort with a hotel, casino, shopping mall, and the famous infinity pool. The SkyPark observation deck offers stunning views (around 32 SGD). The area around Marina Bay is beautiful, especially at night with the light show.

Gardens by the Bay features futuristic Supertree Grove and two climate-controlled conservatories (Flower Dome and Cloud Forest). The outdoor gardens are free, while the conservatories cost around 28 SGD. The light show in the evening is free and spectacular.

Sentosa Island is a resort island with beaches, theme parks, and attractions. It's reached by cable car, monorail, or bridge. Universal Studios Singapore is located here. Entry to the island is free, but individual attractions have fees.

The Singapore Zoo is considered one of the world's best zoos, with open enclosures and excellent animal welfare. Tickets cost around 48 SGD. The Night Safari, adjacent to the zoo, is a unique experience.

Orchard Road is Singapore's main shopping street, lined with malls, hotels, and restaurants. It's a shopper's paradise.

Chinatown offers temples, markets, and excellent food. The Buddha Tooth Relic Temple is impressive and free to visit.

Little India is a vibrant neighborhood with Indian shops, restaurants, and the colorful Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple.

The Singapore Botanic Gardens is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and a beautiful green space. It's free to enter, though the National Orchid Garden within it charges admission (around 15 SGD).

Accommodation:
Singapore offers accommodation for all budgets, though prices are generally high. Hostels start around 25-40 SGD per night. Budget hotels cost 80-150 SGD, mid-range hotels 150-300 SGD, and luxury hotels can exceed 400 SGD per night.

Popular areas to stay include Marina Bay (luxury, iconic), Orchard Road (shopping, central), Clarke Quay (nightlife, riverside), and Little India (budget-friendly, cultural).

Food and dining:
Singapore is a food paradise, with cuisine reflecting its multicultural population. Hawker centers are the heart of Singaporean food culture, offering affordable and delicious meals. A meal typically costs 5-10 SGD.

Must-try dishes include Hainanese chicken rice, laksa (spicy noodle soup), char kway teow (stir-fried noodles), and chili crab. Satay, roti prata, and nasi lemak are also popular.

Restaurants range from hawker centers (5-10 SGD) to mid-range restaurants (30-60 SGD) to fine dining (100-300+ SGD per person).

The city has excellent food from various cultures: Chinese, Malay, Indian, Peranakan, and international cuisines.

Practical tips:
Singapore uses Singapore Dollars (SGD). Credit cards are widely accepted, and contactless payment is very common. Cash is still used, especially at hawker centers.

The best times to visit are year-round, as the climate is consistently warm and humid. However, the dry season (February to April) is slightly more pleasant. Avoid the monsoon season (November to January) if possible, though rain is usually brief.

Singapore is extremely safe, with very low crime rates. However, be aware of strict laws regarding littering, jaywalking, chewing gum (banned), and drug use (death penalty).

The city is very clean, and littering fines are high. Smoking is restricted to designated areas.

Tipping is not expected and sometimes refused. A 10% service charge is often included in restaurant bills.

English is widely spoken and is one of the official languages, along with Malay, Mandarin, and Tamil.

Many attractions offer online booking with discounts. The Singapore Tourist Pass offers unlimited MRT and bus travel plus discounts to attractions.

The city is compact and easy to navigate. Most attractions are accessible by MRT.

Hawker centers are the best places for affordable, authentic food. They're clean, safe, and offer incredible variety.

Alcohol is expensive due to high taxes. Happy hours are common at bars and restaurants.

The weather is hot and humid year-round. Dress lightly, stay hydrated, and take advantage of air-conditioned spaces.
