Paris, the capital of France, is one of the world's most visited cities, known for its art, culture, and iconic landmarks.

Airports:
Paris has two main international airports: Charles de Gaulle (CDG) and Orly (ORY). Charles de Gaulle is the larger airport, located about 25 km northeast of the city center. Orly is smaller and located 13 km south of Paris. Both airports are well-connected to the city center.

Transportation from airports:
From Charles de Gaulle (CDG), the RER B train connects directly to central Paris in approximately 35 minutes. The train stops at major stations including Gare du Nord, Châtelet-Les-Halles, and Saint-Michel-Notre-Dame. Tickets cost around 11.45 euros. Alternatively, Roissybus provides direct bus service to Opéra for 16.20 euros, taking about 60 minutes. Taxis cost approximately 50-60 euros to central Paris.

From Orly (ORY), the Orlyval automated train connects to Antony station, where you can transfer to RER B. The journey takes about 30-35 minutes total. Orlybus provides direct service to Denfert-Rochereau for 11.20 euros. Taxis cost around 35-50 euros to central Paris.

Getting around Paris:
The Paris Metro (Métro) is extensive and covers most areas of the city. It operates from approximately 5:30 AM to 1:15 AM (2:15 AM on weekends). A single ticket costs 2.10 euros, but it's more economical to buy a carnet of 10 tickets for 16.90 euros. The Navigo Easy card is a rechargeable card that works for Metro, buses, and trams.

The RER (Regional Express Network) connects Paris with suburbs and is useful for reaching destinations like Versailles, Disneyland Paris, and the airports. The same Metro tickets work for RER within Paris zones 1-2.

Buses complement the Metro network and are useful for areas not well-served by the Metro. Night buses (Noctilien) operate when the Metro is closed.

Taxis and ride-sharing services like Uber are widely available. Biking is popular with the Vélib' bike-sharing system offering thousands of bikes throughout the city.

Major attractions:
The Eiffel Tower is Paris's most iconic landmark. It's highly recommended to book tickets in advance online, especially during peak season. Tickets for the elevator to the top cost around 29 euros for adults. The tower is open daily from 9:30 AM to 11:45 PM.

The Louvre Museum is the world's largest art museum, home to the Mona Lisa and thousands of other masterpieces. Advance booking is essential to avoid long queues. Tickets cost 17 euros for adults, and the museum is closed on Tuesdays.

Notre-Dame Cathedral, while currently under restoration after the 2019 fire, remains a significant landmark. The exterior can still be viewed.

The Arc de Triomphe offers panoramic views of Paris. Tickets cost 13 euros, and it's located at the western end of the Champs-Élysées.

The Palace of Versailles, located about 20 km southwest of Paris, is a must-see day trip. Take RER C to Versailles-Rive Gauche. The palace and gardens are magnificent, with tickets starting at 18 euros for the palace alone.

Montmartre and the Sacré-Cœur Basilica offer stunning views of the city. The area is known for its artistic history and charming streets.

Accommodation:
Paris has accommodation options for every budget. The Marais, Saint-Germain-des-Prés, and the Latin Quarter are popular central neighborhoods. Budget travelers can find hostels starting around 30-40 euros per night, while mid-range hotels typically cost 100-200 euros. Luxury hotels can exceed 300 euros per night.

Food and dining:
Paris is a culinary capital. Traditional French cuisine includes croissants, baguettes, escargot, coq au vin, and crème brûlée. A meal at a casual bistro typically costs 15-25 euros, while fine dining restaurants can cost 50-150 euros per person. Street food like crêpes and falafel in the Marais are affordable options.

Café culture is central to Parisian life. A coffee at a café typically costs 2-4 euros, though prices are higher at tourist locations.

Practical tips:
Paris is generally safe, but beware of pickpockets in crowded tourist areas, especially on the Metro and near major attractions. Keep valuables secure and be aware of common scams.

English is spoken in tourist areas, but learning a few French phrases (bonjour, merci, s'il vous plaît) is appreciated.

Tipping is not mandatory as service is included, but rounding up or leaving 5-10% for good service is common.

The best times to visit are spring (April-June) and fall (September-October) when weather is pleasant and crowds are smaller. Summer can be hot and crowded, while winter is cold but less crowded.

Many museums offer free entry on the first Sunday of the month, though this can mean longer queues.
